Honest about the maths.

WHOOP measures physiological cycles, not midnight to midnight, so Joul labels today as provisional and marks every balance as an estimate. Days differ — a hard training day burns differently than a rest day, and food swings just as much — so Joul always shows the week next to today, not just one day in isolation. We never assume zero calories burned, and we never treat an empty diary as a perfect day. Joul is not medical advice.

Questions, answered.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account starts with a free trial of full access, photo logging included, with no card needed. The trial is a week, and you can earn more free days by sharing Joul and inviting friends. After that you keep Joul with a subscription: $24.99 a year or $2.99 a month. When you subscribe in the app, Apple may also offer a further free week: you add a payment method, nothing is charged until that week ends, and you can cancel any time before then in your Apple ID settings.

Can I earn more free time?

We're building ways to earn extra days by sharing your week of meals or inviting friends who also use WHOOP. They aren't live yet, and we'll explain the rules in the app when they are.

Do I need a WHOOP?

Yes. Joul's calories-burned number comes from WHOOP, so a WHOOP membership and the WHOOP app are required for the core experience.

Is Joul on Android?

Not at the moment. Joul is being built for iPhone first.

How accurate are the numbers?

They're estimates. WHOOP's energy expenditure is an estimate, and so are portion sizes and nutrition values. Joul shows the balance as an estimate, labels provisional days, and lets you edit everything.

Is this medical advice?

No. Joul is a tracking tool. Talk to a qualified professional about your health, diet and goals.

What happens to my data?

You connect WHOOP through WHOOP's own sign-in. You can disconnect at any time and delete your data from inside the app. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Is Joul affiliated with WHOOP?

No. Joul is an independent app that works with WHOOP data you choose to share. It isn't endorsed by or affiliated with WHOOP.
